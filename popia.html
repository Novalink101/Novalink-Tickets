We are the Responsible Party for the personal information you provide when using Novalink. This means we decide why and how your information is processed, and we are accountable for protecting it.

2. What personal information we process

In summary:

  • Contact and identity: name, email address.
  • Purchase: tickets bought, guest names, seat, price, promo codes used.
  • Event hosting (if you are an Organiser): venue, event details, attendee list.
  • Technical: IP address, browser, device, timestamps.

We do not process special personal information (health, race, religion, political views, trade union membership, sexual orientation, criminal record) as defined in Section 26 of POPIA.

3. Why we process it (purposes)

  • To give you a Novalink account and let you sign in securely.
  • To sell, issue, and verify tickets.
  • To send you transactional emails about your purchases.
  • To support Organisers with attendee lists and event analytics.
  • To process payments and pay out Organisers.
  • To prevent fraud and secure the Platform.
  • To comply with tax and consumer protection laws.

5. Cross-border transfers

Some recipients (Firebase, EmailJS, PayPal) are located in the United States. Section 72 of POPIA permits the transfer of personal information to a foreign country where the recipient is subject to a law, binding agreement, or corporate rules that provide an adequate level of protection substantially similar to POPIA. We rely on those protections. See Section 8 of our Privacy Policy for details.

6. How long we keep it

Broadly:

  • Account data: as long as your account exists, plus 30 days after deletion.
  • Purchase and ticket records: 5 years (tax/financial law).
  • Scan logs: 12 months from the event date.
  • Support messages: 24 months.
  • Technical logs: 90 days.

7. Your rights under POPIA

POPIA gives you the following rights, and we will exercise them on request:

  • Right to access (Section 23) — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction (Section 24) — ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, misleading, or unlawfully obtained.
  • Right to deletion (Section 24) — ask us to delete your information, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Right to object (Section 11(3)) — object to processing on reasonable grounds.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Right to not be subject to automated decisions — we do not currently make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with the Information Regulator (see below).

How to exercise these rights

Email [INFORMATION OFFICER EMAIL] with:

  • Your full name and the email address on your Novalink account.
  • What right you want to exercise (access, correction, deletion, etc.).
  • Any supporting detail.

We will respond within 30 days, as required by POPIA. There is no fee for reasonable requests. We may ask you to verify your identity first, to prevent someone else from accessing your data.

8. Security safeguards

We apply the following safeguards, as required by Section 19 of POPIA:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Password hashing and secure authentication.
  • Role-based access controls on our database.
  • Payment card details handled only by a PCI-DSS-compliant payment provider.
  • Regular review of access logs and Firestore security rules.

9. Data breaches

If we become aware of a security compromise that poses a risk to your personal information, we will notify both you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible, as required by Section 22 of POPIA. Our notification will describe the breach, the likely consequences, and the measures we are taking.
