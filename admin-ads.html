How it works

Viewers see full-screen ads at three moments:

  1. Pre-roll — before the stream loads
  2. Mid-roll — 30 min in by default
  3. Post-roll — when the stream ends

If no advertiser ad is scheduled for a slot, the house ad plays instead.

Ads with matching type (or type "Any") rotate randomly, weighted by their weight value.

Tips

• Best results: Use image URLs hosted by the advertiser. Fast loading, no storage cost.

• Video ads: Keep them short (10–15s). Muted autoplay works on all browsers.

• Scheduling: Set start/end dates so campaigns expire automatically.

• Skip timing: 5 seconds is the industry standard — long enough to register, short enough to not annoy.
