How rotation works

Every stream pulls from the same pool of ads:

  1. Pre-roll — before the stream loads
  2. Mid-roll — timed (default 30 min)
  3. Post-roll — when the stream ends

Ads pick randomly, weighted by their weight value.

Viewers never see the same ad twice in a row (session-scoped memory).
