What you can and cannot stream
Novalink only allows your own original content. If you stream any of the following, your YouTube/Twitch account gets a copyright strike, your stream dies, and your Novalink channel goes offline.
  • Someone else's movie, TV show, or sports match
  • Someone else's YouTube, Twitch, or Facebook live feed
  • Music you don't own the rights to (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music)
  • Nudity, sexual content, or pornography
  • Hate speech, racism, threats, or harassment
  • Real violence, gore, fights, or graphic injuries
  • Terrorism, extremism, weapons, or bomb-making instructions
  • Drug use, drug-selling, or illegal acts
  • Dangerous fake news or medical misinformation
One rule: if you did not create it, do not stream it.

Go live on YouTube in 60 seconds
Pick the option that matches your device. Both end with a URL you paste below.
📱Streaming from your phone
  1. Open the YouTube app on your phone
  2. Tap + → Go Live
  3. Grant camera + mic permission → tap Go Live
  4. Copy the watch link YouTube gives you
  5. Paste it into the URL field below and click Save

Works on any Android or iPhone with the YouTube app installed.

💻Streaming from a PC
  1. Click the button below — YouTube opens in a new tab
  2. Click Go Live in YouTube Studio
  3. Pick your camera or screen share
  4. Copy the watch URL → paste it below and Save

Use Chrome or Edge on desktop.

Go live on Twitch
Stream from the Twitch app or OBS. Then paste your channel link below.
📱Streaming from your phone
  1. Open the Twitch app on your phone
  2. Tap your profile → Go Live
  3. Grant camera + mic permission → tap Go Live
  4. Copy your channel link (twitch.tv/yourname)
  5. Paste it below and click Save

Viewers will need to tap once to start the stream — that's Twitch's policy.

💻Streaming from a PC
  1. Stream to Twitch using OBS or Twitch Studio
  2. When live, copy your channel URL
  3. Paste it below and click Save

Paste the channel link, not the dashboard link.

Vimeo Live
Paste your Vimeo video or live event URL below.
🔗Vimeo link format
  1. Go to your Vimeo live event page
  2. Copy the URL — it should look like vimeo.com/123456789
  3. Paste it below and click Save

Vimeo Live requires a paid plan on Vimeo's side.

Facebook Live
Paste the link to your Facebook live video.
🔗How to get the link
  1. Go live on Facebook
  2. Tap the Share button on your live video
  3. Copy the video link (facebook.com/.../videos/...)
  4. Paste it below and click Save

Facebook requires viewers to be logged in to see your stream.

Custom Embed
Paste any embeddable URL — from your own site, a partner platform, or a streaming service.
🌐Works with
  1. Self-hosted streams (RTMP → HLS)
  2. Other platforms (Kick, Rumble, Dailymotion)
  3. Custom player URLs from event companies
  4. Anything that allows embedding

If the source blocks embedding, the player will show a blank screen.
