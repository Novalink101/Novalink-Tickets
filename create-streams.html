What you can and cannot stream
Novalink only allows your own original content. Streaming someone else's content risks a copyright strike, kills the stream, and takes your channel offline.
  • Someone else's movie, TV show, or sports match
  • Someone else's YouTube, Twitch, or Facebook live feed
  • Music you don't own the rights to
  • Nudity, sexual content, or pornography
  • Hate speech, racism, threats, or harassment
  • Real violence, gore, fights, or graphic injuries
  • Drug use, drug-selling, or illegal acts
  • Dangerous fake news or medical misinformation
One rule: if you did not create it, do not stream it.
