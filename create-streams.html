Broadcast your stream
Pick the option that matches your device. Both end with a URL you paste below.
📱Playing on your phone
  1. Open the YouTube app on your phone
  2. Tap + → Go Live → pick your game
  3. Grant screen + audio permission → tap Go Live
  4. Copy the watch link YouTube gives you
  5. Paste it into the URL field below and click Save

Browsers can't capture your phone's screen — YouTube's app is the only way.

💻Playing on PC / laptop
  1. Click the button below — YouTube opens in a new tab
  2. Click Go Live in YouTube's studio
  3. Chrome asks what to share → pick Entire Screen
  4. Tick Share audio at the bottom of that popup
  5. Copy the watch URL → paste it below and Save
