Who we are

Novalink exists to make events secure, simple and honest — for organisers and guests alike.

About us

Novalink is a South African digital ticketing platform. We design event cards, verify every guest at the door, and handle the payment flow between buyers, organisers and our own systems — cleanly and transparently.

Our mission

To eliminate ticket fraud, lost revenue and gate chaos in African events. Every card we generate is unique, every scan is logged, and every organiser gets paid what they earned.

Our vision

A continent where any organiser — from a 30-person church supper to a 50,000-person festival, from a local party to a multi-day trip — can run a fully digital, secure, verified event with the same tools the biggest venues use.

What we do, what we don't

Clarity matters. Here is exactly where we operate and where we do not.

What we do

  • Design and generate digital event cards
  • Verify every guest at the door with QR and barcode
  • Revoke cards on duplicate scans — no double entry
  • Handle payments from buyers to organisers via PayPal
  • Provide attendance lists and no-show flagging
  • Support long-distance trips with destination cards
  • Match card colours to the event theme
  • Log every scan for audit and dispute resolution

What we don't do

  • We do not issue physical tickets or print cards
  • We do not allow card downloads — cards stay in your account
  • We do not handle bookings on WhatsApp — bookings happen on the site
  • We do not store card or banking details — PayPal handles that
  • We do not sell or share your personal data
  • We do not refund flagged no-shows
  • We do not support cash payments
  • We do not accept events without a named organiser

Long-distance trips

Trip cards are built for journeys that span days or weeks. Each card carries images of the destination, a description, departure and return dates.

Destination images

Upload photos of the destination so travellers know what to expect. Images appear on the card and on the event page.

Multi-day dates

Set departure and return dates separately. Trip cards display the full span, not just a single day.

Full description

Itinerary, packing notes, meeting points, transport details — all stored on the trip card and visible to the traveller.

Important disclaimers
Payment processingTicket payments are processed by PayPal in USD. The ZAR price is displayed for reference and is converted at the current rate at the time of checkout.
Processing feeA processing fee is added at checkout. This fee covers payment processing, currency conversion and bank withdrawal costs. It does not affect the organiser's revenue.
Editing and cancellationEvents can be edited freely until 12 hours before the event starts. Within the last 12 hours, R20 per field changed applies. Cancellation within 12 hours carries a R150 flat fee.
Flagged cardsAttendees flagged as not attending are not refundable. This is set by the organiser and recorded on the card.
RefundsRefunds are investigated per reference number and card number. If a Novalink system failure caused the issue, a partial or full refund is issued. All refund decisions are made after investigation.
Screenshot preventionNovalink cannot prevent screenshots of a card on a device. Cards revoke on the second scan at the door. Sharing a screenshot will not allow entry.
Scanned cardsOnce a card is scanned at the door it is marked as checked-in. A second scan revokes the card. All scans are logged with a timestamp and cannot be edited or deleted.
Card ownershipEvery card belongs to the person named on it. Cards cannot be transferred, sold or reassigned. Cards are issued per ticket, not per person.
Data and privacyCard and attendee data is stored on Firebase. Organisers can see only their own events. Admin access is limited to the registered admin account. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.
Account and event deletionDeleting an event removes it permanently along with all its attendee cards. Deleting your account removes all events you own and all cards issued under them. This action cannot be undone.
Platform availabilityNovalink runs on Render and Firebase. In the unlikely event of an outage, entry is handled manually using the scanned card list. We are not liable for delays caused by third-party service interruptions.
