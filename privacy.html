3. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Issue tickets, send confirmation emails, and generate QR codes.
  • Verify tickets at the door and prevent duplicate or fraudulent entry.
  • Process payments (via our payment provider) and calculate platform fees.
  • Support Organisers with attendee lists and event analytics (guest names, scan status, revenue).
  • Send transactional emails (purchase confirmations, ticket cancellations, event changes).
  • Respond to support requests and complaints.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations, including tax and consumer protection laws.
  • Improve the Platform (aggregated, de-identified analytics only).

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for advertising retargeting.

4. Lawful basis for processing (POPIA)

Under POPIA, we process your information on the following bases:

  • Consent — where you have given us clear permission (e.g., agreeing to these terms when creating an account).
  • Contract — to perform the ticket purchase and event hosting contracts you enter into via the Platform.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax, consumer protection, and other laws.
  • Legitimate interest — to prevent fraud, secure the Platform, and improve our service, in a way that does not override your rights.

6. How long we keep it

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law:

  • Account data — while your account is active, plus 30 days after you delete it.
  • Ticket and purchase records — 5 years from the date of purchase, to comply with tax and financial record-keeping laws.
  • Scan logs — 12 months from the event date, to resolve disputes and prevent fraud.
  • Support communications — 24 months from the last message.
  • Technical logs — 90 days.

When retention periods end, we delete or irreversibly anonymise the information.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) on all connections to and from the Platform.
  • Password hashing and secure authentication via Firebase Authentication.
  • Firestore security rules restricting access to data based on user roles.
  • Access controls on internal systems and least-privilege principles.
  • Payment card data is handled entirely by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment provider; we never see or store full card numbers.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by Section 22 of POPIA.

8. Cross-border transfers

Because we use Firebase (Google LLC), EmailJS, and payment providers located in the United States, some of your information is transferred outside South Africa. Section 72 of POPIA allows such transfers where the recipient is subject to a law, binding agreement, or corporate rules providing an adequate level of protection substantially similar to POPIA. We rely on:

  • Google's standard contractual clauses and data processing terms.
  • EmailJS's data processing terms.
  • Payment providers' contractual data protection commitments.

Where possible, we limit the amount of personal information transferred and prefer pseudonymised identifiers.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The Platform uses the following:

  • Essential cookies — required to keep you signed in and to secure requests (Firebase Authentication). These cannot be disabled.
  • Local storage — small pieces of data stored in your browser (for example, your scanner sound preference). These stay on your device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not currently use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies. If we add them in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update information that is inaccurate, misleading, or out of date.
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to our legal retention obligations).
  • Object to processing on reasonable grounds.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis for processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

See our POPIA notice for how to exercise these rights.

11. Children

You must be at least 18 to create a Novalink account. If you are under 18 and want to attend an event, the ticket must be purchased by a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us immediately at [INFORMATION OFFICER EMAIL].
